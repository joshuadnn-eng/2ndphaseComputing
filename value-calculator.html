Questions

How is the value worked out?

We start with what you paid for it new. Most tech loses about 10% of its value each year, so we take that off for every year you've had it, and then adjust for condition. The result is a guide to what it could sell for today.

Why is the shop price so much lower?

Shops, pawn shops and trade-in services usually offer about a quarter of what an item is worth, sometimes a little more. They have to test, clean, warranty and resell it, and they carry the risk if something is wrong. Selling privately gets you more, but takes longer.

Does it work for any item?

Yes. Laptops, PCs, phones, tablets, TVs, consoles, parts and more. Some items, like popular gaming parts or Apple products, can hold their value better, so ask us for an exact figure.
