Questions

How is the value worked out?

We looked at what similar devices are selling for second-hand in South Africa, from private sellers, pre-owned shops and refurbishers. The calculator uses the type of device, its age, processor, memory, storage, graphics and condition to give you a likely price range.

Why is the shop price lower than the private price?

Shops and trade-in services pay less because they test, clean, warranty and resell the device, and they carry the risk if something is wrong with it. Selling privately usually gets you more, but takes longer.

Can you give me an exact figure?

Yes. Send us the details and a few photos on WhatsApp, or bring it in to 135 De Wet St, Krugersdorp, and we'll take a look and give you an honest figure.

My device has a fault. Is it worth fixing before I sell?

Often, yes. Things like a new battery, a screen or an SSD upgrade can add more value than they cost. Tell us what's wrong and we'll tell you if it's worth it.
